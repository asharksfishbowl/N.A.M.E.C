# Spec: Combat & Loot

## Overview
Combat is deliberate and Souls-like: stamina-gated attacks, dodge rolls with invulnerability frames, blocking, parrying, lock-on, executions of weakened enemies, and punishing bosses that gate each climate region. Loot is Diablo-style: each player receives their own drops, and items roll rarity tiers and random affixes. Downed players can be revived by teammates. Shared terms are defined in `specs/game-foundation/game-foundation.md`.

## Goals
- Combat rewards timing and positioning over button-mashing.
- Stats, class abilities, skills, and gear each visibly change combat outcomes.
- Every boss is a memorable encounter whose defeat unlocks the next tier of progression.
- Loot drops are exciting because of random rarity and stats, and co-op players never compete for drops.

## Non-Goals
- PvP modes.
- Mounted combat.
- Turn-based or dice-roll hit resolution. Hits are physical, determined by hitboxes.
- Item trading UI (players drop items on the ground to share).

## Requirements

### Core Combat
1. Melee attacks resolve via weapon hitbox traces during attack animation windows (anim notifies), on the server. Bow shots resolve as server-spawned projectiles (Requirement 53).
2. Actions and default stamina costs (from `DT_Survival_StaminaCosts`): light attack, off-hand light attack (Requirement 59), heavy attack, dodge roll, block (per hit absorbed), sprint (per second), bow shot (per shot, aimed or quick; Requirement 55). Spells are class abilities, and their mana and stamina costs come from `DT_Progression_ClassAbilities` (see character-progression spec).
3. A dodge roll grants invulnerability frames for a window defined in `DT_Combat_Movement`. The window and roll recovery time depend on the equip load tier. Equip load is computed per `specs/inventory/inventory.md` Requirement 6, and the tiers are: Light (<30%), Medium (30% to <70%), Heavy (70% to ≤100%), Overloaded (>100%: cannot dodge roll). An Over-Encumbered character also cannot dodge roll.
4. Blocking (gamepad LT, keyboard right mouse button; see `specs/game-foundation/game-foundation.md` Requirement 14) with the blocking item chosen by Requirement 59 reduces incoming damage by that item's block percentage (halved for a shield or weapon whose category no held class allows, Requirement 36, and multiplied by EmptyOffHandBlockMultiplier for a Right Hand weapon blocking with an empty Left Hand, Requirement 59), reduces incoming poise damage (Requirement 38), and drains stamina per hit. A bow can't block (Requirement 53), a stowed Left Hand item can't block (Requirement 54), torches and tools can't block, and a character with a one-handed weapon in the Left Hand can't block (Requirement 59). When stamina hits 0 while blocking, the guard breaks and the player is staggered.
5. Parry (gamepad LB + LT, keyboard Left Alt + right mouse button; see `specs/game-foundation/game-foundation.md` Requirement 14) uses the parry item chosen by Requirement 59 and is available only when that item is parry-capable (shields, some weapons). Parry is not available with a bow in the Right Hand (Requirement 53), and a stowed Left Hand item never parries (Requirement 54). A parry within the parry window staggers the attacker and opens a riposte (critical hit) window.
6. Lock-on targets the hostile closest to screen center among hostiles within 20 m of the player (tuning value). While locked on, the camera frames the target and movement strafes. The lock-on input (gamepad R3, keyboard Tab) toggles lock-on, and flicking the right stick (gamepad) or the mouse (keyboard) while locked on cycles to the next target in the flick direction (see `specs/game-foundation/game-foundation.md` Requirement 14).
7. Poise: characters and enemies have a Poise value. Hits deal poise damage, and at 0 Poise the target staggers. Poise regenerates after 3 seconds without taking hits (tuning value).
8. Damage formula: `Damage = (WeaponBase + StatScaling + AffixFlat) × (1 + SkillBonus + AffixPercent) × (1 − TargetResistance) × RequirementPenalty × (1 − WeakenedPenalty) × CritMultiplier × TwoHandingMultiplier × HeavyAttackMultiplier`. HeavyAttackMultiplier is HeavyAttackDamageMultiplier (Requirement 58) for a heavy attack hit, otherwise 1. RequirementPenalty is the product of every Requirement 36 multiplier (starting value 0.5 each) that applies: one when the attacker is below the weapon's stat requirements, one when the weapon's category is allowed by no held class, and, for a class ability, one when the caster is below its RequiredStat and RequiredValue. When none applies, RequirementPenalty is 1. WeakenedPenalty is the Requirement 17 damage-dealt penalty (starting value 0.2) while Weakened, otherwise 0. CritMultiplier applies only to a player's riposte critical hits (Requirement 5), otherwise 1, and its value lives in `DT_Combat_Rules` (tuning value). Enemies never land critical hits (Requirement 38). TwoHandingMultiplier is 1.25 (tuning value) for weapon hits and non-spell class abilities while the attacker is two-handing a one-handed weapon (Requirement 54), otherwise 1. For a bow shot, WeaponBase is the bow's WeaponBase plus the fired arrow's ArrowDamage (Requirement 53). For spells, WeaponBase is the ability's base damage from `DT_Progression_ClassAbilities`. StatScaling uses the weapon's scaling stat modifier(s) and scaling grade from the item definition. While the attacker is two-handing a one-handed weapon (Requirement 54), the STR modifier's contribution to StatScaling is multiplied by 1.5 (tuning value). For spells, StatScaling uses the INT modifier (Destruction, Arcane) or the WIS modifier (Restoration, Nature), with coefficients in `DT_Progression_DerivedStats`. SkillBonus comes from the skill of the weapon's category (One-Handed, Two-Handed, or Archery; `specs/character-progression/character-progression.md` Requirement 29) or the spell's magic school. A non-spell class ability deals `AbilityDamage = WeaponDamagePercent × Damage`, where WeaponDamagePercent comes from the ability's row in `DT_Progression_ClassAbilities` (e.g., 1.5) and Damage is this full formula computed with the Right Hand weapon's WeaponBase, StatScaling, and SkillBonus. While a bow is equipped, a non-spell class ability that deals damage counts as a bow shot (Requirement 53), so its WeaponBase is the bow's WeaponBase plus the fired arrow's ArrowDamage. When the Right Hand slot is empty or holds a tool or torch, the Unarmed weapon profile in `DT_Combat_Rules` is used instead (a tool swing's own hit on an enemy follows Requirement 56): its WeaponBase (tuning value), STR as the scaling stat, One-Handed as the skill, and Blunt as the damage type. A non-spell ability does not use the ability's base damage.
9. Damage types: Physical (Slash, Pierce, Blunt), Fire, Frost, Lightning, Poison, Holy, Shadow. Enemies and armor have resistances per type. Every weapon item definition has exactly one damage type. A weapon hit (including an off-hand light attack, Requirement 59) and a non-spell class ability deal the damage type of the weapon used, a bow shot (including a non-spell class ability used with a bow, Requirement 53) deals the bow's damage type, and a hit with the Unarmed weapon profile (Requirement 8) or a tool swing (Requirement 56) deals Blunt. A spell deals the damage type in its `DT_Progression_ClassAbilities` row (`specs/character-progression/character-progression.md` Requirement 16). Players hold one resistance attribute per damage type plus an Armor attribute on `UNamecAttributeSet`, and enemies hold an Armor attribute and the per-type resistances on `UNamecEnemyAttributeSet`. Each equipped item's Armor value and resistance values add to the wearer's attributes, including resistance affixes. TargetResistance in Requirement 8 is: for Slash, Pierce, or Blunt damage, `Armor ÷ (Armor + ArmorConstant)` plus the matching subtype resistance attribute; for every other type, the matching resistance attribute. An enemy's Armor is 0 unless it has equipped items (`specs/enemy-ai/enemy-ai.md` Requirement 30), and the same TargetResistance formula applies to enemies. TargetResistance is capped at 0.9. ArmorConstant (starting value 100) and the cap are tuning values.
10. Status effects: Poison (damage over time), Bleed (burst damage when a buildup meter fills), Burn (Fire damage over time), Frostbite (stamina regen reduction). Buildup and durations live in `DT_Combat_StatusEffects`. Every status-effect buildup a player receives is multiplied by (1 − StatusResistance), using the `StatusResistance` attribute (`specs/character-progression/character-progression.md` Requirement 6). Poison, Bleed, Burn, and Frostbite buildup and active effects carry the gameplay tag `Status.Negative.Cleansable` (removable by Sauren Shed Skin, `specs/character-creation/character-creation.md` Requirement 13). Stagger is not a buildup status effect; Stagger comes only from Poise reaching 0 (Requirement 7) and from guard break (Requirement 4) or parry (Requirement 5).
11. Class abilities, including spells, are GAS abilities that consume mana and/or stamina, with cooldowns, defined per class. Mana cost, stamina cost, cooldown, base damage (spells), and WeaponDamagePercent (non-spell abilities; Requirement 8) come from `DT_Progression_ClassAbilities` (see character-progression spec).
12. Friendly fire is a world setting, off by default. When off, player attacks, spells, and area effects never damage other players. When on, they do. Player attacks, spells, and area-of-effect abilities never damage building pieces under either setting (`specs/voxel-world/voxel-world.md` Requirement 26).

### Downed & Revive
13. When a player's health reaches 0, the player enters the Downed state instead of dying: the player can crawl slowly, cannot attack or use items, and a 30-second bleed-out timer starts (tuning value). Damage taken while Downed does not reduce health. Instead, each hit reduces the remaining bleed-out timer by 1 second per 1% of max health the hit would have dealt (tuning value).
14. A teammate reviving holds the interact input for 4 seconds within 2 m of the downed player (tuning values). Taking damage interrupts the revive. The Cleric class ability "Revivify" revives instantly within 15 m (tuning value).
15. A revived player returns at 30% max health (tuning value).
16. When a downed player's bleed-out timer ends, that player dies. When every player in the session is downed or dead, all downed players die. A player counts as dead from the moment of death until respawn (the Requirement 17 respawn delay).
17. On death: the player respawns 5 seconds after death (tuning value) at their bed in this world, or at the world spawn point when no bed is set or the bed has been destroyed (`specs/survival/survival.md` Requirement 18), with full health, full Mana, 50% Hunger and Thirst, BodyTemperature 37 °C, Fatigue 0, and a 5-minute "Weakened" debuff (−20% damage dealt, −20% max stamina). Weakened reduces damage dealt only and never reduces healing (Requirement 39). Equipment durability loses 10% (tuning values). Jewelry has no durability and is unaffected (`specs/inventory/inventory.md` Requirement 26). No items are dropped, and no gold is lost.
18. In a single-player session (one player total), the Downed state is skipped. Health 0 means immediate death.

### Enemies
19. Enemies are defined in `DT_Combat_Enemies` with: category (`Hostile`, `Wildlife`, `Boss`), Faction (`None`, `Bandits`, `Beastmen`, or a kingdom faction ID; `specs/factions-kingdoms/factions-kingdoms.md` Requirements 2 and 3), skeleton family (a row of `DT_EnemyAI_SkeletonFamilies`, whose Humanoid flag decides whether the enemy equips picked-up items, `specs/enemy-ai/enemy-ai.md` Requirement 26; also used by executions, Requirement 47), level, health, poise, resistances, damage, attack set (each attack with a `UsesWeapon` flag, `specs/enemy-ai/enemy-ai.md` Requirement 30), perception radius, FleeHealthThreshold (optional, `specs/enemy-ai/enemy-ai.md` Requirement 8), loot table ID (including the gold range for `Hostile` rows, Requirement 33), Hunting yield (optional), region, KillXP (character XP per kill), FirstKillXP (character XP per boss first-kill, set only for `Boss` rows; see character-progression spec Requirement 8), and AI StateTree asset (StateTree and Smart Objects per `specs/engine-tech/engine-tech.md` Requirement 3; states and behaviour in `specs/enemy-ai/enemy-ai.md` Requirements 1–11). `Wildlife` creatures never attack, and flee from every player, enemy, and town NPC they detect or that damages them (`specs/enemy-ai/enemy-ai.md` Requirement 8). Each enemy owns a `UAbilitySystemComponent` with `UNamecEnemyAttributeSet` (Health, MaxHealth, Poise, MaxPoise, Armor, resistances).
20. Enemy spawns come from the `DT_Combat_Enemies` rows whose category is `Hostile` or `Wildlife`, whose Faction is `None`, and whose region column matches the spawn location's region, filtered by time of day (some enemies are night-only). Bandit and Beastmen rows spawn only at their camps and in raids, and kingdom raider rows spawn only in raids (`specs/factions-kingdoms/factions-kingdoms.md` Requirements 18 and 56). Spawn density and the maximum live enemies per streamed chunk come from `DT_Combat_Spawning`. Enemies never spawn within 25 m of any placed building piece, within 40 m of any player (tuning values), or inside a town's protected radius (`specs/factions-kingdoms/factions-kingdoms.md` Requirement 15). Raiders are exempt from the building piece distance, spawn density, and per-chunk cap rules, and keep the 40 m player distance rule (`specs/factions-kingdoms/factions-kingdoms.md` Requirement 56). Camp spawn points are exempt from the building piece distance, player distance, spawn density, and per-chunk cap rules, so a respawned camp always refills (`specs/factions-kingdoms/factions-kingdoms.md` Requirement 18). `Boss` rows never spawn through normal enemy spawning: a boss appears only when summoned at its arena altar (Requirement 23). Every newly spawned enemy starts at its base level with 0 XP and no picked-up items (`specs/enemy-ai/enemy-ai.md` Requirement 42). Veterans spawn from their saved records, exempt from these spawn rules and caps (`specs/enemy-ai/enemy-ai.md` Requirement 47).
21. Enemy health and damage scale with the number of players in the session per `DT_Combat_PlayerScaling` (starting values: +60% health and +10% damage per additional player). Player-count scaling does not apply to town NPCs, including escort NPCs (`specs/factions-kingdoms/factions-kingdoms.md` Requirement 22).
22. Enemies detect and aggro players, and every other target hostile to them by the hostility matrix (`specs/enemy-ai/enemy-ai.md` Requirements 13–17), per Requirement 41. Enemy aggro targets the entry in its threat table with the highest threat. Threat comes from damage dealt, healing done, taunt abilities, and detection (`specs/enemy-ai/enemy-ai.md` Requirement 7). A taunt is a forced-target effect: while it lasts, the enemy targets the taunting character regardless of threat, and when it ends, the enemy's normal threat table resumes. Taunt durations are set by the taunting ability (e.g., Mauling Roar, `specs/character-creation/character-creation.md` Requirement 13).

### Bosses
23. Each region has one boss in a fixed arena location (placed by world generation). A boss is summoned by placing a region-specific offering item at the arena altar. Offering items drop from that region's `Hostile` enemies at the rate set in `DT_Loot_Tables`. Each eligible player rolls for an offering independently as part of their per-player loot.
24. Bosses have named multi-phase fights with telegraphed attacks. Each boss's phases and attack set are defined in its own Blueprint subclass of `ANamecBossBase`.
25. When a boss fight starts, the arena is enclosed by a barrier. Players inside at summon time are locked in until the boss dies or every player inside is dead. Players outside cannot enter.
26. Defeating a boss sets `BossDefeated[RegionId] = true` in `UNamecWorldSave`. Each of the 7 region bosses before the Volcanic region drops a guaranteed boss trophy and that boss's own distinct tier material (see voxel-world spec Requirement 8) for **each player present** (each player inside the arena barrier when the boss dies). The tier material is required to craft station upgrades and gear for the next tier (see crafting-jobs spec). Each player present whose character lacks that boss's first-kill flag receives FirstKillXP instead of KillXP, including in re-summoned fights (Requirement 27). Every other player present receives KillXP (see character-progression spec Requirement 8). The final (Volcanic) boss instead drops a guaranteed Legendary item plus a trophy for each player present, gates nothing, and is re-summonable as endgame content (Requirement 27).
27. Defeated bosses can be re-summoned for more loot. Re-summoning uses a new offering item.

### Loot
28. Loot is per-player instanced: when an enemy dies or a loot chest opens, each eligible player gets independently rolled drops visible and pickable only by that player. Ownership is per player (character GUID), not per connection, because split-screen players share one connection: the server replicates each per-player loot actor only to its owner's connection (`bOnlyRelevantToOwner`), the actor renders only in the owning local player's viewport (`bOnlyOwnerSee` on its primitives, owned by that player's PlayerController), so other local players on the same machine, including the host, never see it, and the server accepts a pickup only from the owning player. Loot chests are placed by world generation (see voxel-world spec Requirement 10) and are distinct from crafted storage containers. Each player can open each loot chest once, with an independent per-player roll. The opened state is tracked per character GUID in `UNamecWorldSave`. Loot chests do not refill. A per-player loot actor left unclaimed for 2 real-time minutes (tuning value in `DT_EnemyAI_Rules`) converts into shared world pickups visible to every player and lootable by enemies (`specs/enemy-ai/enemy-ai.md` Requirement 20).
29. Eligible = within 50 m of the enemy at death, or dealt damage to it (tuning value). For a `Boss` enemy, the only eligible players are the players present inside the arena barrier when the boss dies (Requirement 26), for both loot and XP, and the 50 m and dealt-damage rules do not apply. For a loot chest, the only eligible player is the player who opens it. A player who executes an enemy (Requirement 44) is always eligible for that kill. Every player eligible for an enemy kill also receives that enemy's full KillXP (`specs/character-progression/character-progression.md` Requirement 8), adjusted for the enemy's current level and the Veteran bonus (`specs/enemy-ai/enemy-ai.md` Requirements 39 and 51).
30. Rarity tiers and max affix counts: Common (0), Magic (1–2), Rare (3–4), Epic (4–5), Legendary (5 + one unique Legendary power). Rarity weights come from `DT_Loot_RarityWeights`, keyed by item-level band, for both enemy drops and crafted items, and are modified by any Magic Find affix. Jewelry (`specs/inventory/inventory.md` Requirement 26) rolled by `UNamecLootSubsystem` for an enemy drop or a loot chest never rolls Common: the Common weight is excluded and the rarity rolls from the remaining weights, so the item has at least 1 affix.
31. Item level = source enemy's current level (`specs/enemy-ai/enemy-ai.md` Requirement 38), or for a loot chest a uniform random integer within the item-level band of the chest's region in `DT_World_Climates`, rolled per player, or crafted item level (see crafting-jobs spec). Affix value ranges scale with item level per `DT_Loot_Affixes`.
32. Affix pool examples: +STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA, +% damage of a type, +resistance, +max health, +stamina regen, +Insulation/Cooling, +skill XP gain for a skill, +Magic Find, life on hit. No affix appears twice on one item.
33. Loot tables (`DT_Loot_Tables`) define item categories and drop counts for each enemy and chest type, and a gold range for each `Hostile`-category enemy (including Bandits, Beastmen, and raiders) and each chest type. `Wildlife` and `Boss` rows have no gold range. Gold is rolled per eligible player within the range and placed in that player's per-player loot actor, and picking the loot actor up adds the gold to the player's gold counter (`specs/factions-kingdoms/factions-kingdoms.md` Requirement 28).
34. An item dropped on the ground by a player (via inventory) becomes a shared world pickup any player can see and take, and any `Hostile`-category enemy can pick up (`specs/enemy-ai/enemy-ai.md` Requirement 19). Gold dropped with the Drop Gold action (`specs/factions-kingdoms/factions-kingdoms.md` Requirement 30) also becomes a shared world pickup, and picking it up adds its amount to the picker's gold.
35. Equipment has durability, reduced by use. Exception: jewelry has no durability, never reaches 0, and needs no repair (`specs/inventory/inventory.md` Requirement 26). Durability 0 makes the item unusable (not destroyed). Repairing at the station of the item's crafting Job restores full durability and costs 25% of the item's recipe materials, rounded up (tuning value). Dropped items with no recipe use the recipe of their base item type. Alternatively, a Repair Kit (Blacksmith recipe, required Job level 1) repairs any item to full durability at any crafting station, with no Job level requirement, consuming one Repair Kit. The station repair path above remains the alternative for craftable items.
36. Equipment may have stat requirements (e.g., STR 16). Equipping below requirements is allowed, but the item deals 50% damage or gives 50% of its Armor and resistance values (Requirement 9) (tuning value). The same penalty value applies to a weapon or armor piece whose category no held class allows, and multiplies the block percentage of a shield or weapon whose category no held class allows (see character-progression spec Requirements 15, 28, and 30), and to a class ability's damage and healing when the caster is below the ability's RequiredStat and RequiredValue (see character-progression spec Requirement 16). When several of these penalties apply to the same hit or item, they multiply (e.g., 0.5 × 0.5 = 25%).

### Tuning Data
37. Every value marked "tuning value" in this spec that names no other table lives in `DT_Combat_Rules`.

### Incoming Damage, Poise Damage & Healing
38. Damage an enemy attack deals to a player: `IncomingDamage = (EnemyDamage + EquippedWeaponBase) × LevelDamageMultiplier × PlayerScaling × (1 − TargetResistance) × (1 − BlockPercent)`. EnemyDamage and the damage type are the attack's values in the enemy's attack set in `DT_Combat_Enemies`. EquippedWeaponBase is the WeaponBase of the enemy's equipped Right Hand weapon when the attack's `UsesWeapon` flag is set, and that attack then deals the weapon's damage type; otherwise EquippedWeaponBase is 0 (`specs/enemy-ai/enemy-ai.md` Requirement 30). LevelDamageMultiplier is DamagePerLevel raised to the enemy's gained levels (`specs/enemy-ai/enemy-ai.md` Requirement 39), and 1 for a Guard. The same formula, with BlockPercent 0, gives an enemy's or Guard's damage to a non-player target (`specs/enemy-ai/enemy-ai.md` Requirement 16). PlayerScaling is the Requirement 21 damage multiplier for the session's player count (locked at summon for bosses; Edge Case 4). TargetResistance is the player's Requirement 9 value for that damage type. BlockPercent is the blocking item's block percentage (Requirement 4) while the player is blocking, otherwise 0. Enemy attacks, including Guard attacks, never land critical hits: IncomingDamage has no critical multiplier, and CritMultiplier (Requirement 8) applies only to player riposte critical hits. For a Guard attack on a player (Requirement 42), EnemyDamage, the damage type, and `PoiseDamage` are the attack's values in the Guard's attack set in `DT_Factions_NPCs` (`specs/factions-kingdoms/factions-kingdoms.md` Requirement 20), EquippedWeaponBase is 0, and PlayerScaling is 1. Poise damage per hit comes from: the weapon's `PoiseDamage` on its item definition for weapon hits (the Unarmed weapon profile's `PoiseDamage` in `DT_Combat_Rules` when the Right Hand is empty or holds a tool or torch, tuning value), multiplied by HeavyAttackPoiseMultiplier (Requirement 58) for a heavy attack hit; the ability's `PoiseDamage` column in `DT_Progression_ClassAbilities` for class abilities; the racial active ability's poise damage in `DT_Character_Races` for racial abilities (`specs/character-creation/character-creation.md` Requirement 12); and the attack's `PoiseDamage` in `DT_Combat_Enemies` for enemy attacks. While a player is blocking, the poise damage that player takes from a hit is multiplied by BlockPoiseMultiplier, 0.5 (tuning value). A player's MaxPoise = base MaxPoise (tuning value in `DT_Progression_DerivedStats`) + the total `Poise` value of every equipped armor piece's item definition. An enemy's MaxPoise = the poise column of its `DT_Combat_Enemies` row × PoisePerLevel raised to its gained levels (`specs/enemy-ai/enemy-ai.md` Requirement 39) + the total `Poise` value of its equipped armor pieces (`specs/enemy-ai/enemy-ai.md` Requirement 30).
39. Healing from a class ability: `Healing = (BaseHealing + StatScaling) × (1 + SkillBonus) × RequirementPenalty`. BaseHealing is the ability's `BaseHealing` column in `DT_Progression_ClassAbilities`. StatScaling uses the WIS modifier, with the coefficient in `DT_Progression_DerivedStats`. SkillBonus comes from the ability's magic school when it is a spell, otherwise 0. RequirementPenalty is the Requirement 36 multiplier when the caster is below the ability's RequiredStat and RequiredValue, otherwise 1. Healing never raises Health above MaxHealth. The Weakened debuff (Requirement 17) reduces damage dealt only and never reduces healing, so the healing formula has no Weakened term.

### Fall Damage
40. A player or enemy that lands after falling a vertical distance at or above the minimum fall height takes fall damage = (fall distance − minimum fall height) × fall damage per meter, as a percentage of max health. Fall distance is measured from the highest point of the fall to the landing point. Fall damage ignores Armor, resistances, and blocking. The minimum fall height and fall damage per meter are tuning values. Racial exemptions are in `specs/character-creation/character-creation.md` Requirement 12 (Felari Night Eyes).

### Enemy Perception
41. A `Hostile` enemy detects and aggroes a player, or any other target hostile to it by the hostility matrix (`specs/enemy-ai/enemy-ai.md` Requirements 15 and 17), who is inside its perception radius and in its line of sight. The perception radius starts at the enemy's perception radius in `DT_Combat_Enemies` (Requirement 19). Crouching halves it, the Stealth skill reduces it further (`specs/character-progression/character-progression.md` Requirement 21), and a racial perception multiplier then multiplies the final radius (Hundari Loud ×1.25, `specs/character-creation/character-creation.md` Requirement 12). The crouch, Stealth, and racial modifiers apply only to player targets. A target that is perceived but not detected starts Investigate (`specs/enemy-ai/enemy-ai.md` Requirement 6).

### Town NPCs & Raiders
42. Town NPCs (Guards, Guard Captains, Vendors, Quest Givers, Citizens; `specs/factions-kingdoms/factions-kingdoms.md` Requirements 20–26) are `ANamecTownNPC` actors, a subclass of `ANamecEnemyBase`, with a `UAbilitySystemComponent` and `UNamecEnemyAttributeSet` whose values come from `DT_Factions_NPCs`. Player hits on town NPCs use the Requirement 8 formula (tool swings use Requirement 56), and Guard attacks on players use the Requirement 38 formula. Town NPCs award no skill XP, class XP, or KillXP, drop no loot or gold, and cannot be executed. Player attacks damage town NPCs regardless of the friendly-fire setting (Requirement 12). Lock-on (Requirement 6) also targets a Guard that is currently attacking the locking player. `Hostile` enemies detect, aggro, and attack the town NPCs and escort NPCs that the hostility matrix makes hostile to them (`specs/enemy-ai/enemy-ai.md` Requirement 13; kingdom raiders attack Guards but not the Town NPC group) with the Requirement 22 and Requirement 41 rules they use for players.
43. Raider targeting: a raider (`specs/factions-kingdoms/factions-kingdoms.md` Requirement 56) targets the nearest standing building piece in its raid's building piece snapshot (`specs/factions-kingdoms/factions-kingdoms.md` Requirement 58), including crafting stations, storage containers, beds, and doors, and moves to the next nearest piece in the snapshot when that piece is destroyed. Raiders cannot open doors, and a closed door's navmesh area is impassable for them, so a raider gets through a closed door only by destroying it (`specs/voxel-world/voxel-world.md` Requirement 45). A raider that takes damage from a player adds that player to its threat table and targets the player with the highest threat until that player is Downed, dead, or outside the raider's perception radius, then returns to the pieces in the snapshot. A hostile enemy or Guard that damages a raider is added to its threat table the same way (`specs/enemy-ai/enemy-ai.md` Requirement 10). A taunt (Requirement 22) forces the target as usual. Raider attacks reduce building piece health (`specs/voxel-world/voxel-world.md` Requirement 26). Enemies that are not raiders never target building pieces.

### Executions
44. An execution prompt appears for a player when an enemy in the `Hostile` category of `DT_Combat_Enemies` (including Bandits, Beastmen, raiders, and Veterans, `specs/enemy-ai/enemy-ai.md` Requirement 43; not `Boss`, not `Wildlife`, and never a town NPC) is within 2.5 m (tuning value) and inside the player's forward execution angle (tuning value), and that enemy is either below 20% of its MaxHealth (tuning value) or inside the riposte window opened by that player's own parry (Requirement 5). The player must also be able to execute: not Downed or dead, not already in an execution, on the ground (not airborne, climbing, or swimming), and with no menu or interaction screen open. Any menu or interaction screen counts, for example the Inventory and Character menu, the Favorites quick menu, the pause menu, the Hammer build menu (`specs/voxel-world/voxel-world.md` Requirement 41), or a station, hand-crafting, container, dye, Mirror, vendor, quest board, quest giver, or Guard Captain fine screen (`specs/factions-kingdoms/factions-kingdoms.md` Requirement 65). When several enemies qualify, the prompt uses the player's locked-on target when it qualifies, otherwise the closest qualifying enemy.
45. The execution prompt shows only in the owning local player's viewport. A per-local-player Settings option "Execution prompts" (on by default) hides the prompt and disables executions for that local player when turned off. The option is saved per local player slot in `UNamecSettingsSave` (`specs/game-foundation/game-foundation.md` Requirement 6).
46. The Execute input is Y (gamepad) or R (keyboard) and works only while the prompt is shown. While the prompt is shown, Y and R execute instead of toggling two-handing, and LB + Y still casts ability 4 (`specs/game-foundation/game-foundation.md` Requirement 14). An execution costs no stamina.
47. An execution plays a paired attacker and victim animation, aligned with Motion Warping per `specs/engine-tech/engine-tech.md` Requirement 3, chosen from `DT_Combat_Executions`, keyed by the attacker's Right Hand weapon category and the victim's skeleton family (Requirement 19). The Unarmed set is used when the Right Hand is empty or holds a bow, a tool, or a torch. When `DT_Combat_Executions` has no row for the weapon category and skeleton family pairing, the generic set for that skeleton family is used.
48. On the Execute input, the client sends `ServerRequestExecution(TargetId)`. The server validates the Requirement 44 conditions (including the attacker conditions), range, that the enemy is not already being executed and is not airborne, and that a capsule sweep finds clear space for the paired animation's attacker and victim positions. When validation fails, the execution does not start and the prompt hides.
49. During the execution animation, the attacker and the victim are invulnerable to all damage, and neither can be interrupted, staggered, or moved by other sources. Other enemies keep acting. The victim dies at the animation's kill notify, regardless of its remaining health. The kill counts as the attacker's kill for loot eligibility (Requirement 29), KillXP, and quest credit (`specs/factions-kingdoms/factions-kingdoms.md` Requirement 44).
50. When the victim dies, the attacker restores 20% of MaxStamina (tuning value) and receives weapon skill XP equal to 3× (tuning value) the XP of one normal hit on that enemy (`specs/character-progression/character-progression.md` Requirements 21 and 22), for One-Handed whenever the Unarmed set is used (Requirement 47), including with a bow in the Right Hand, and otherwise for the skill a light attack with the attacker's Right Hand weapon trains.
51. When two players request an execution on the same enemy, the first request the server receives wins, and every later request is rejected and hides that player's prompt.
52. Executions work in first-person and third-person. In first-person, the camera switches to a third-person execution camera (a PlayerCameraManager camera modifier per `specs/engine-tech/engine-tech.md` Requirement 3) for the animation and switches back to first-person when the animation ends.

### Bows & Arrows
53. With a bow in the Right Hand, the aim input (gamepad LT, keyboard right mouse button) aims instead of blocking, because a bow can't block, and the fire input (gamepad RB, keyboard left mouse button) fires an aimed shot while aiming and a quick shot while not aiming (Requirement 55; `specs/game-foundation/game-foundation.md` Requirement 14). Parry (Requirement 5) is not available with a bow. Bow shots follow these rules:
    - Each shot consumes 1 arrow from the Ammo slot (`specs/inventory/inventory.md` Requirement 12). With an empty Ammo slot the bow can't fire, and "No arrows" shows in the owning local player's viewport only.
    - Every arrow item definition has an `ArrowDamage` value. A shot's damage is the Requirement 8 formula with WeaponBase = the bow's WeaponBase + the fired arrow's `ArrowDamage`. Arrows are Carpenter recipes (`specs/crafting-jobs/crafting-jobs.md` Requirement 1), and the Carpenter Job-level potency of `specs/crafting-jobs/crafting-jobs.md` Requirement 11 scales `ArrowDamage`. Looted and bought arrows have Job level 1 potency (`specs/crafting-jobs/crafting-jobs.md` Requirement 11).
    - The server spawns the projectile and resolves its hits. The firing client may play a cosmetic predicted projectile, which never deals damage or resolves a hit.
    - Each projectile hit that deals damage to a hostile enemy trains Archery (`specs/character-progression/character-progression.md` Requirement 21).
    - A non-spell class ability that deals damage (Requirement 8), used while a bow is equipped, counts as a bow shot: its WeaponBase is the bow's WeaponBase + the fired arrow's `ArrowDamage`, it consumes 1 arrow from the Ammo slot, and the server spawns its projectile and resolves its hits like any other shot. With an empty Ammo slot the ability can't be activated, and "No arrows" shows in the owning local player's viewport only. Its damage is WeaponDamagePercent × that shot's Requirement 8 damage, with no QuickShotDamageMultiplier. It costs the ability's own mana and stamina costs (Requirement 11), not the bow shot stamina cost.

### Two-Handing
54. The two-handing toggle input (gamepad Y, keyboard R, when no execution prompt is shown; `specs/game-foundation/game-foundation.md` Requirement 14) switches a one-handed weapon in the Right Hand between one-handed and two-handed use. While a one-handed weapon is two-handed:
    - The Left Hand item is stowed. It stays equipped in the Left Hand slot data and still counts toward equip load (`specs/inventory/inventory.md` Requirement 6), but grants none of its effects while stowed.
    - Weapon damage is multiplied by TwoHandingMultiplier, 1.25, and StatScaling's STR contribution by 1.5 (tuning values; Requirement 8).
    - Hits still train the weapon's own skill, One-Handed (`specs/character-progression/character-progression.md` Requirement 21).
    
    Toggling back un-stows the Left Hand item. Two-handed items (two-handed weapons and bows) are always two-handed, and the toggle does nothing for them. Shields can't be two-handed.

### Bow Handling, Tool Hits & Two-Handing State
55. A bow is a two-handed item that occupies both hand slots (`specs/inventory/inventory.md` Requirement 12), so no Left Hand item can be equipped with it. With a bow equipped:
    - The fire input while not aiming fires a quick shot: a Requirement 53 shot whose damage is multiplied by QuickShotDamageMultiplier, 50% of an aimed shot's damage (tuning value). A quick shot fires along the camera's aim direction, or toward the locked-on target while locked on (Requirement 6), with no other aim assist. It follows every other Requirement 53 rule (arrow consumption, "No arrows", server-resolved projectile, Archery XP).
    - The heavy attack input (gamepad RT, keyboard middle mouse button) does nothing.
    - Each shot, aimed or quick, costs stamina, with the bow shot cost in `DT_Survival_StaminaCosts` (tuning value; `specs/survival/survival.md` Requirement 15).
56. Tools are axes, pickaxes, shovels, Hammers, and Fishing Rods (`specs/voxel-world/voxel-world.md` Requirements 12, 13, and 41; `specs/character-progression/character-progression.md` Requirement 29), and have no weapon category. Every tool except the Fishing Rod has a `WeaponBase` on its item definition. With a Fishing Rod in the Right Hand, the light attack input casts instead of swinging (`specs/game-foundation/game-foundation.md` Requirement 14), so a Fishing Rod never makes a tool swing and has no `WeaponBase`. Every rule that names a tool in the Right Hand (Requirements 8, 47, 58, and 59) includes the Fishing Rod. With a tool or a torch in the Right Hand, no heavy attack happens (Requirement 58). A tool swing that hits an enemy in the `Hostile` (including Bandits, Beastmen, and raiders), `Boss`, or `Wildlife` category of `DT_Combat_Enemies` deals ToolEnemyDamageMultiplier, 50% (tuning value), of the Requirement 8 damage computed with the tool's `WeaponBase` in place of the Unarmed weapon profile's WeaponBase and the rest of the Unarmed weapon profile (STR scaling, Blunt damage type, and its `PoiseDamage`, Requirement 38), with SkillBonus 0. Tool swings that hit an enemy award no skill XP of any kind: no weapon skill XP and no gathering skill XP (`specs/character-progression/character-progression.md` Requirement 21). Non-spell class abilities with a tool in the Right Hand still use the Unarmed weapon profile (Requirement 8). A tool swing that hits a town NPC (Requirement 42) counts as a player attack: it deals the same tool hit damage, costs the same reputation (`specs/factions-kingdoms/factions-kingdoms.md` Requirement 8), and draws the same Guard reaction (`specs/factions-kingdoms/factions-kingdoms.md` Requirement 23) as any attack, and awards no skill XP.
57. Two-handing (Requirement 54) ends automatically, un-stowing the Left Hand item, when the Right Hand weapon changes (another item is equipped into the Right Hand, or the Right Hand is emptied) or when an item is equipped into the Left Hand. Two-handing state is not saved: every character loads into a world with its Right Hand weapon held one-handed (`specs/game-foundation/game-foundation.md` Requirement 6).

### Heavy Attacks
58. A heavy attack (the heavy attack input, gamepad RT, keyboard middle mouse button; `specs/game-foundation/game-foundation.md` Requirement 14) is a weapon attack with the Right Hand weapon, or with the Unarmed weapon profile when the Right Hand is empty. It costs the heavy attack stamina cost in `DT_Survival_StaminaCosts` (Requirement 2) and resolves like a light attack (Requirement 1), with these differences:
    - Its damage is the Requirement 8 formula with HeavyAttackMultiplier = HeavyAttackDamageMultiplier, 1.6 (tuning value).
    - Its poise damage is the Requirement 38 weapon hit poise damage multiplied by HeavyAttackPoiseMultiplier, 2.0 (tuning value).
    - It trains the same skill a light attack with the same Right Hand weapon trains (`specs/character-progression/character-progression.md` Requirement 21).
    
    With a bow (Requirement 55), a tool (axe, pickaxe, shovel, Hammer, or Fishing Rod; Requirement 56), or a torch in the Right Hand, no heavy attack happens: with a shovel or Hammer the input performs its context action instead (`specs/game-foundation/game-foundation.md` Requirement 14), and with a bow, axe, pickaxe, Fishing Rod, or torch it does nothing. Non-spell class abilities never use HeavyAttackMultiplier.

### Dual-Wielding & Block Source
59. A one-handed weapon of any weapon category except Staff can be equipped in the Left Hand (`specs/inventory/inventory.md` Requirement 12). What the block input (gamepad LT, keyboard right mouse button) does, which item blocks (Requirement 4), and which item parries (Requirement 5) follow the first rule below that matches the hands:
    1. **Bow in the Right Hand:** LT aims (Requirement 53), and parry is not available.
    2. **Two-handed:** while the Right Hand holds a two-handed weapon (Two-Handed Sword, Two-Handed Axe, or Two-Handed Hammer category) or a one-handed weapon being two-handed (Requirement 54), LT blocks with that weapon at its block percentage (Requirement 4, without EmptyOffHandBlockMultiplier), and parry uses that weapon when it is parry-capable. A stowed Left Hand item never blocks, parries, or attacks.
    3. **One-handed weapon in the Left Hand:** LT performs an off-hand light attack with the Left Hand weapon instead of blocking, and parry uses the Left Hand weapon when it is parry-capable. The character cannot block.
    4. **Shield in the Left Hand:** LT blocks with the shield at its block percentage, and parry uses the shield when it is parry-capable.
    5. **Torch in the Left Hand:** LT does nothing and the character cannot block. Parry uses the Right Hand weapon when it is parry-capable, as with an empty Left Hand (rule 6).
    6. **Empty Left Hand:** LT blocks with the Right Hand weapon at its block percentage × EmptyOffHandBlockMultiplier, 0.5 (tuning value), and parry uses the Right Hand weapon when it is parry-capable. With the Right Hand empty or holding a tool or torch, there is no blocking item: LT does nothing and parry is not available.
    
    Torches and tools never block or parry, in either hand. An off-hand light attack resolves like a light attack (Requirement 1) with the Left Hand weapon's hitbox and costs the off-hand light attack stamina cost in `DT_Survival_StaminaCosts` (tuning value). Its damage is the Requirement 8 formula computed with the Left Hand weapon's WeaponBase, StatScaling, SkillBonus (the skill of the Left Hand weapon's category), and RequirementPenalty multipliers, with TwoHandingMultiplier 1 and HeavyAttackMultiplier 1. Its poise damage is the Left Hand weapon's `PoiseDamage` (Requirement 38), and its hits train the skill of the Left Hand weapon's category (`specs/character-progression/character-progression.md` Requirement 21). Heavy attacks (Requirement 58), executions (Requirement 47), and non-spell class abilities (Requirement 8) always use the Right Hand, never the Left Hand weapon.

### Torch Bash
60. A light attack with a torch in the Right Hand is a torch bash: an Unarmed light attack using the Unarmed weapon profile (Requirement 8) and its `PoiseDamage` (Requirement 38), which also applies Burn buildup on each hit at the torch bash Burn buildup value in `DT_Combat_StatusEffects` (tuning value), multiplied by the target's StatusResistance when the target is a player (Requirement 10). Torch bash hits train One-Handed like other Unarmed hits (`specs/character-progression/character-progression.md` Requirement 21). A light attack with a tool in the Right Hand stays a tool swing, or a cast with a Fishing Rod (Requirement 56).

## Data Flow
1. Player presses light attack → client predicts the animation via GAS ability `GA_LightAttack` → server validates stamina, commits cost, and plays the montage.
2. The anim notify opens the hitbox window → server traces the weapon hitbox → for each hit hostile, the server builds a damage gameplay effect spec using the Requirement 8 formula and applies it.
3. Target's attribute set subtracts health and poise → the server applies stagger if Poise ≤ 0 and awards weapon skill XP to the attacker via the progression component.
4. At enemy health 0 → server determines eligible players → awards full KillXP to each eligible player via the progression component (at the enemy's current level, `specs/enemy-ai/enemy-ai.md` Requirement 39) → the enemy drops its picked-up items and gold as shared world pickups (`specs/enemy-ai/enemy-ai.md` Requirement 32) → `UNamecLootSubsystem` rolls a drop set per eligible player → spawns per-player loot actors owned by each eligible player's PlayerController, replicated only to the owner's connection and rendered only in the owner's viewport (Requirement 28).
5. At player health 0 → server checks session player count → Downed state or death → on revive or respawn, applies the effects in Requirements 15/17.
6. Boss death → server sets the defeat flag, awards FirstKillXP to each player inside the arena barrier whose character lacks that boss's first-kill flag (including in a re-summoned fight) and sets the flag, triggers the world save, and rolls guaranteed trophies per present player.
7. Player opens a loot chest → server checks the opener's character GUID against the chest's opened set → `UNamecLootSubsystem` rolls a drop set for the opener only → server spawns the owner-only loot actors and records the GUID in the chest's opened set.
8. An enemy meets the Requirement 44 conditions → the owning client's `UNamecExecutionComponent` shows the prompt in that player's viewport → the player presses Execute → client sends `ServerRequestExecution(TargetId)` → server validates per Requirement 48 → server marks the victim as being executed, applies invulnerability to both, and plays the `DT_Combat_Executions` paired montages (switching a first-person attacker to the execution camera) → at the kill notify the server kills the victim and runs Data Flow 4 with the attacker included as eligible → server restores the attacker's stamina and awards weapon skill XP (Requirement 50).
9. Player with a bow equipped presses fire, while aiming for an aimed shot or while not aiming for a quick shot (Requirement 55) → client sends the shot through `GA_BowFire` and may play a cosmetic predicted projectile → server validates the stamina cost and that the Ammo slot holds an arrow, commits the stamina cost, removes 1 arrow, and spawns the replicated `ANamecProjectile` with the bow and arrow values → on a server-resolved hit, the server applies the Requirement 8 damage with WeaponBase = bow WeaponBase + `ArrowDamage` (× QuickShotDamageMultiplier for a quick shot) and awards Archery XP for a damaging hit on a hostile enemy. With an empty Ammo slot, no shot fires and the owning viewport shows "No arrows".

## Edge Cases
1. When a player disconnects cleanly while downed (or a host local player leaves while downed), the character saves as dead-respawn: on next join they spawn per `specs/multiplayer/multiplayer.md` Requirement 16 (bed, or world spawn point) with the death effects from Requirement 17. Any character save written while the character is Downed or dead (autosave, disconnect, or exit, including host exit) sets the dead-respawn flag instead of saving the character at 0 health, and the flag clears once the death effects are applied on the next world entry. The dead-respawn flag takes precedence over the saved Health and Mana (`specs/game-foundation/game-foundation.md` Requirement 6): the character enters with the Requirement 17 respawn state (full Health and full Mana), not its saved Health and Mana. When a remote client's connection drops unexpectedly while downed, no final save is sent, and the character keeps its last autosave state (`specs/multiplayer/multiplayer.md` Requirement 17).
2. When a player's per-player loot is left unclaimed for 2 real-time minutes, it converts into shared world pickups that every player can see and take and enemies can pick up (`specs/enemy-ai/enemy-ai.md` Requirement 20). Each converted pickup despawns 10 minutes (tuning value) after the original per-player loot actor spawned, not after the conversion.
3. When all players inside a boss arena die, the boss resets to full health and the barrier drops.
4. When a player disconnects inside a boss arena, their character is removed from the fight, and boss scaling does not change mid-fight. Player-count scaling is locked at summon.
5. When a player joins the session mid-combat, existing enemies keep their current scaling. Newly spawned enemies use the new player count.
6. When a dodge roll's invulnerability frames overlap an area-of-effect tick, the tick is ignored for that player.
7. When friendly fire is off, a falling tree log still damages players (environmental damage, not player damage).
8. When a Legendary unique power has no valid effect for the item's slot, `DT_Loot_Affixes` excludes it from that slot's pool, so an invalid roll never happens.
9. When another player's attack, a raider, or an area-of-effect tick hits an execution victim during the animation, the hit deals no damage, because the victim is invulnerable (Requirement 49). The victim always dies at the kill notify of the attacker's execution.
10. When an enemy attacks the executing player during the animation, the hit deals no damage, so the attacker cannot become Downed mid-execution.
11. When the enemy stands on a building piece, on a slope, or next to a wall, the Requirement 48 capsule sweep decides: the execution starts only when both animation positions are clear, otherwise the prompt hides.
12. When two players request an execution on the same enemy in the same frame, the request the server receives first wins and the other is rejected (Requirement 51).
13. When the enemy's health rises back to 20% or more of MaxHealth, or the riposte window ends, before the Execute input, the prompt hides and a request sent afterward fails validation.
14. When the attacker disconnects or is removed mid-animation, the victim leaves the executed state, loses its invulnerability, and resumes at its current health.
15. When a player has "Execution prompts" turned off, no prompt shows for that player and Y or R toggles two-handing, while prompts for other local players on the same machine are unaffected.
