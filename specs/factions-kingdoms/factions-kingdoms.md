# Spec: Factions, Kingdoms, Economy & Raids

## Overview
The world holds six race kingdoms, each with a capital, towns, NPCs, vendors, and quest boards, plus two always-hostile factions, Bandits and Beastmen, who live in camps. Each character earns and loses reputation with every faction, earns and spends gold, takes quests, and defends player bases against random raids that arrive with no warning. Shared terms are defined in `specs/game-foundation/game-foundation.md`.

## Goals
- Towns give players a place to trade, take quests, and build standing with each race's kingdom.
- Reputation visibly changes how a kingdom treats a character: vendor prices, services, questline access, guard hostility, and raids.
- Gold gives loot and gathering a use beyond equipment and crafting.
- Player bases are worth defending, because a raid can arrive at any time a player is nearby.

## Non-Goals
- NPC daily schedules, NPCs travelling between towns, or dialogue trees beyond the vendor, quest board, quest giver, and Guard Captain fine screens.
- Player-to-player trading UI, or gold transfer other than dropping gold as a world pickup (Requirement 30).
- Changing Bandits or Beastmen reputation.
- Wars between kingdoms, player-owned towns, or claiming territory.
- Raid warnings, scheduled raids, or raids on bases with no player nearby.
- Haggling. Prices follow Requirements 32 and 33.

## Requirements

### Factions
1. Eight factions exist, each a row in `DT_Factions_Factions` with: faction ID, display name, type (`Kingdom` or `Outlaw`), race ID from `DT_Character_Races` (Kingdom only), home region (Kingdom only), camp density per region (Outlaw only), camp respawn time (Outlaw only), and camp radius (Outlaw only, tuning value; Requirement 17).
   - **Kingdoms:** the Human, Felari, Hundari, Sauren, Vanari, and Ursan kingdoms, one per playable race (`specs/character-creation/character-creation.md` Requirement 12). Kingdom display names in `DT_Factions_Factions` are placeholders.
   - **Outlaws (always hostile):** Bandits and Beastmen.
2. Every row in `DT_Combat_Enemies` has a Faction column (`specs/combat-loot/combat-loot.md` Requirement 19) holding `None`, `Bandits`, `Beastmen`, or a kingdom's faction ID. Region monsters (the existing `Hostile`, `Wildlife`, and `Boss` rows) have Faction `None`, belong to no faction, and have no reputation. Every row with Faction `Bandits`, `Beastmen`, or a kingdom is a `Hostile`-category row, so every rule that applies to `Hostile` enemies also applies to Bandits, Beastmen, and kingdom raiders.
3. Rows with a kingdom Faction are **kingdom raiders**. Kingdom raiders spawn only in raids (Requirement 56) and never through normal enemy spawning (`specs/combat-loot/combat-loot.md` Requirement 20).

### Reputation
4. Each character holds one reputation value per faction, an integer from −100 to +100. Every change is clamped to that range.
5. Reputation tiers:

   | Tier | Reputation |
   |------|-----------|
   | Hostile | −100 to −50 |
   | Unfriendly | −49 to −10 |
   | Neutral | −9 to +9 |
   | Friendly | +10 to +49 |
   | Honored | +50 to +100 |

6. A new character starts with reputation +30 with the kingdom of the character's race, 0 with the other five kingdoms, and −100 with Bandits and Beastmen (starting values). Bandits and Beastmen reputation is fixed at −100 and no source changes it.
7. Reputation with a kingdom increases only through quest rewards (Requirement 45), Hostile recovery (Requirement 63), and paying a Guard Captain's fine (Requirement 65).
8. Reputation with a kingdom decreases when a player harms one of that kingdom's NPCs (Requirement 20):
   - When damage from the player's attack, spell, class ability, racial ability, or area-of-effect ability reaches a Guard, Guard Captain, Vendor, Quest Giver, or Citizen, the player loses 10 reputation with that NPC's kingdom (starting value). This loss applies once per NPC per player, and applies again only after that NPC has taken no damage from that player for a cooldown (tuning value).
   - When the player kills a Guard or Citizen, the player loses a further 25 reputation with that kingdom (starting value).
   - Damage to kingdom NPCs is not affected by the friendly-fire world setting (`specs/combat-loot/combat-loot.md` Requirement 12).
9. Tier effects, applied per character for each kingdom:
   - **Hostile:** that kingdom's Guards attack the character on sight (Requirement 23). That kingdom's Vendors, Quest Givers, and Quest Boards refuse service and show "[Kingdom] refuses to deal with you" in the owning local player's viewport. That kingdom can raid bases (Requirement 53). The character's reputation with that kingdom recovers over time (Requirement 63), and the kingdom's Guard Captain accepts a fine that ends the Hostile tier (Requirement 65).
   - **Unfriendly:** vendor prices × 1.25.
   - **Neutral:** vendor prices × 1.00.
   - **Friendly:** vendor prices × 0.90.
   - **Honored:** vendor prices × 0.80, and the kingdom's questline finale can be accepted (Requirement 47).
   
   The price multipliers are starting values in `DT_Factions_Rules`.
10. A character's tier is recomputed on every reputation change and takes effect immediately: Guards update their hostility toward that character, and an open Vendor, Quest Board, or Quest Giver screen of that kingdom closes when the character's tier becomes Hostile.
11. Reputation is stored in `UNamecCharacterSave` (`specs/game-foundation/game-foundation.md` Requirement 6), travels with the character like the rest of the character (`specs/multiplayer/multiplayer.md` Requirement 16), and is the same in every world.
12. The quest log (Requirement 48) has a Reputation section listing each of the eight factions with its reputation value and tier.

### Kingdoms & Towns
13. Each kingdom has exactly one capital and 2 towns. World generation places them deterministically (`specs/voxel-world/voxel-world.md` Requirement 6) inside the kingdom's home region:

    | Kingdom | Home region |
    |---------|-------------|
    | Human | Temperate Forest & Meadows |
    | Sauren | Swamp |
    | Hundari | Savanna |
    | Felari | Desert |
    | Vanari | Jungle |
    | Ursan | Tundra |

    The Snowy Mountains and Volcanic regions have no kingdom and no towns. In this spec, "town" means a capital or a town unless the text says otherwise.
14. Capitals and towns are structure placements defined in `DT_World_Structures` (`specs/voxel-world/voxel-world.md` Requirement 10). Generation places each one so that its protected radius (Requirement 15) does not overlap another town's protected radius, a boss arena's radius, or the world spawn point's building-blocked zone (`specs/voxel-world/voxel-world.md` Requirement 28). Town buildings are part of the structure placement, not building pieces: they cannot be deconstructed or damaged, and they never count toward a base (Requirement 51).
15. Each town has a protected radius (tuning value), measured horizontally from the town's center, so the protected area extends from the world floor to the sky. Inside it, terrain cannot be dug, filled, or mined (`specs/voxel-world/voxel-world.md` Requirement 37), players cannot place building pieces (`specs/voxel-world/voxel-world.md` Requirement 28), and enemies do not spawn (`specs/combat-loot/combat-loot.md` Requirement 20).
16. Each town's `DT_World_Structures` row lists its Quest Board position and its NPC posts: fixed positions, each naming one NPC row in `DT_Factions_NPCs`. Every town has at least one Quest Board, one Vendor, and one Quest Giver, and every capital has exactly one Guard Captain (Requirement 64).

### Camps
17. Bandit camps and Beastmen camps are structure placements defined in `DT_World_Structures`. World generation places them deterministically in every region at that faction's per-region camp density in `DT_Factions_Factions` (a density of 0 places no camps), outside every town protected radius, boss arena radius, and the world spawn point's building-blocked zone. Each camp has a camp radius (the faction's camp radius in `DT_Factions_Factions`, tuning value), measured horizontally from the camp's center like a town's protected radius (Requirement 15). Inside it, players cannot place building pieces (`specs/voxel-world/voxel-world.md` Requirement 28), checked by the same protected radius check the server uses for town protected radii. Terrain inside a camp radius can still be dug, filled, and mined (`specs/voxel-world/voxel-world.md` Requirement 38).
18. Each camp's `DT_World_Structures` row lists enemy spawn points and loot chest positions. When a camp's chunk streams in, each spawn point whose enemy is not dead spawns one enemy, picked at random from the `DT_Combat_Enemies` rows whose Faction matches the camp's faction and whose region matches the camp's region (a data validator guarantees at least one such row, Requirement 62). Camp enemies do not count toward the spawn density or per-chunk caps in `DT_Combat_Spawning`, and camp spawn points are exempt from the 25 m building piece and 40 m player distance rules of `specs/combat-loot/combat-loot.md` Requirement 20, so every spawn point whose enemy is not dead spawns its enemy and a respawned camp always refills. Camp loot chests follow the loot chest rules in `specs/combat-loot/combat-loot.md` Requirement 28 (opened once per character, never refilled).
19. A camp is **cleared** when the enemy of every spawn point in that camp is dead. A cleared camp respawns the enemies of all its spawn points after the faction's camp respawn time in `DT_Factions_Factions` (starting value 1 in-game day), measured in in-game time (`specs/voxel-world/voxel-world.md` Requirement 11). Each camp's per-spawn-point dead states, cleared state, and respawn timer are stored in `UNamecWorldSave`.

### NPCs
20. Town NPCs have one of five roles: Guard, Vendor, Quest Giver, Citizen, or Guard Captain (Requirement 64). Each NPC is a row in `DT_Factions_NPCs` with: NPC ID, role, kingdom, race ID, sex, appearance preset, level, health, poise, resistances, attack set (Guard only; each attack with damage, damage type, and `PoiseDamage`), perception radius, skeleton family, AI StateTree asset (`specs/engine-tech/engine-tech.md` Requirement 3), vendor stock list ID (Vendor only), and respawn time (Guard and Citizen only).
21. Each NPC stands at its fixed post in its town (Requirement 16). NPCs have no daily schedules. An NPC that moves during combat or while fleeing returns to its post afterward.
22. Town NPCs use the same GAS combat as enemies: each is an `ANamecTownNPC` (a subclass of `ANamecEnemyBase`) with a `UAbilitySystemComponent` and `UNamecEnemyAttributeSet`, takes damage by `specs/combat-loot/combat-loot.md` Requirement 8, and deals damage to players by `specs/combat-loot/combat-loot.md` Requirement 38. Town NPCs are not rows in `DT_Combat_Enemies` and are not hostile enemies for any rule: they award no skill XP, no class XP, and no KillXP, drop no loot and no gold, cannot be skinned, and cannot be executed (`specs/combat-loot/combat-loot.md` Requirement 44). Enemy player-count scaling (`specs/combat-loot/combat-loot.md` Requirement 21) does not apply to town NPCs, including escort NPCs: their health and damage are the `DT_Factions_NPCs` values in every session size.
23. A Guard attacks, on detection by `specs/combat-loot/combat-loot.md` Requirement 41 (perception radius and line of sight):
    - any player whose reputation tier with the Guard's kingdom is Hostile,
    - any raider (Requirement 56),
    - any `Hostile`-category enemy inside the town's protected radius,
    - any player who damaged that Guard, until that player is outside the town's protected radius, Downed, or dead.
    
    A Guard does not pursue a target beyond the town's protected radius. A Guard's attacks damage only the targets listed above. Exception: after a player pays a Guard Captain's fine, Guards of that kingdom within 20 m of that Guard Captain (tuning value) do not attack that player for 60 s (tuning value) (Requirement 65).
24. A Citizen that takes damage flees from its attacker like `Wildlife` (`specs/combat-loot/combat-loot.md` Requirement 19). Vendors, Quest Givers, and Guard Captains cannot be killed: their health never drops below 1, and after each hit they cower and refuse interaction for the cower duration (tuning value). Damage to a Vendor, Quest Giver, or Guard Captain still costs reputation (Requirement 8).
25. A killed Guard or Citizen respawns at its post after its respawn time in `DT_Factions_NPCs` (starting value 1 in-game day), measured in in-game time. Each town NPC's respawn timer is stored in `UNamecWorldSave`. NPC health is not saved: every living NPC is at full health when the world loads.
26. `Hostile`-category enemies detect, aggro, and attack town NPCs and escort NPCs (Requirement 40) with the same perception and threat rules they use for players (`specs/combat-loot/combat-loot.md` Requirements 22 and 41).
27. The Interact input (`specs/game-foundation/game-foundation.md` Requirement 14) on a Vendor opens the vendor screen, on a Quest Giver opens the quest giver screen, on a Quest Board opens the quest board screen, and on a Guard Captain opens the fine screen (Requirement 65). Each screen opens in the owning local player's viewport and does not pause the world. A screen closes when the player becomes Downed or moves out of interaction range (tuning value), or when the NPC starts cowering. Two or more players can use the same NPC or Quest Board at the same time, each with an independent screen.

### Gold & Vendors
28. **Gold** is a per-character integer currency counter from 0 to 2,147,483,647. Gold is not an inventory item: it has 0 weight, does not appear in the inventory list, and is shown in the inventory footer (`specs/inventory/inventory.md` Requirement 11). Gold gains above the maximum are clamped. Gold is stored in `UNamecCharacterSave` and is not lost on death (`specs/combat-loot/combat-loot.md` Requirement 17).
29. Gold comes from: `Hostile`-category enemy kills, including Bandits, Beastmen, and raiders, through the gold range of the loot table, rolled per eligible player like other loot (`specs/combat-loot/combat-loot.md` Requirement 33); loot chests, rolled per opening player; quest rewards (Requirement 45); and selling items to Vendors (Requirement 32).
30. A player can drop gold from the inventory footer with the Drop Gold action, choosing an amount up to the character's gold. The amount leaves the counter and becomes one shared world pickup at the player's location (`specs/combat-loot/combat-loot.md` Requirement 34). Any player who picks it up adds the amount to that player's gold. Gold pickups are saved with the other shared world pickups in `UNamecWorldSave`.
31. Every item definition has a `Value` in gold, an integer of 0 or more (`specs/inventory/inventory.md` Requirement 21).
32. Selling: a Vendor buys an item for `floor(Value × 0.25 × RarityMultiplier)` gold per unit, where 0.25 is the sell rate (tuning value) and RarityMultiplier is the item instance's rarity multiplier: Common ×1, Magic ×1.5, Rare ×2.5, Epic ×4, Legendary ×8 (tuning values). An item instance with no rolled rarity (for example consumables, materials, and dye items) uses the Common multiplier. Affixes, durability, CHA, and reputation do not change the sell price. Items with `Value` 0, quest items (`specs/inventory/inventory.md` Requirement 24), and equipped items cannot be sold. A sold item is removed from the seller's inventory and is not added to the Vendor's stock.
33. Buying: a Vendor sells an item for `max(1, round(Value × (1 − CHA modifier × 0.03) × TierMultiplier))` gold per unit, where the CHA modifier is the buyer's (`specs/character-progression/character-progression.md` Requirements 3 and 5), 0.03 is the CHA price coefficient (tuning value), and TierMultiplier is the buyer's Requirement 9 price multiplier for the Vendor's kingdom. The Requirement 32 rarity multiplier does not apply to buy prices. Vendor equipment is always Common (Requirement 34), so the multiplier would be ×1 and have no effect there.
34. Each Vendor's stock comes from the rows of `DT_Factions_VendorStock` with that Vendor's stock list ID. Each row has: stock list ID, item definition, quantity per restock, and item level (equipment only). Bought equipment is Common rarity with no affixes, at the row's item level and full durability. Stock is per world and shared by every player in the session. Every Vendor restocks every in-game day at 06:00 in-game (tuning value), resetting each row to its quantity per restock, including when a sleep time skip passes 06:00 (`specs/survival/survival.md` Requirement 19). Current stock quantities are stored in `UNamecWorldSave`.
35. Vendors sell no boss materials (`specs/voxel-world/voxel-world.md` Requirement 8). An editor data validator (Data Validation, `specs/engine-tech/engine-tech.md` Requirement 3) fails the content build when a `DT_Factions_VendorStock` row names a boss material.
36. The vendor screen shows two lists (the player's sellable items | the Vendor's stock), each row's price for this player, the stock quantity left, and the player's gold. The server validates range, reputation tier, gold, stock quantity, and item ownership on every transaction. When two players buy the last units of a stock row, the server processes requests in receive order and rejects the portion that exceeds the remaining stock.

### Quests
37. Each town's Quest Board offers between 3 and 5 quests (tuning values). At each refresh the board rolls the offer count uniformly within that range and rolls each offer from the `DT_Factions_QuestTemplates` rows of the board's kingdom. Every Quest Board refreshes every in-game day at 06:00 in-game (tuning value), replacing all offers, including when a sleep time skip passes 06:00. Each offer has a unique offer GUID and an accepted character GUID set (Requirement 42). Board offers, with their accepted character GUID sets, are stored in `UNamecWorldSave`.
38. Each quest template row in `DT_Factions_QuestTemplates` has: template ID, kingdom, quest type, objective parameter ranges for that type (Escort and Deliver templates have no destination column: the destination town slot is rolled per Requirements 40 and 41), gold reward range, reputation reward, character XP reward, item reward chance, and item reward list.
39. Quest types and objectives:
    - **Hunt** — kill N of a named `Hostile`-category row in `DT_Combat_Enemies`.
    - **Gather** — deliver N of a named item definition. The items are removed from the player's inventory on turn-in.
    - **Clear Camp** — clear (Requirement 19) any camp of a named Outlaw faction in a named region.
    - **Escort** — lead an escort NPC to another town of the same kingdom (Requirement 40).
    - **Deliver** — carry a quest item to a Quest Giver in another town of the same kingdom (Requirement 41).
    
    N and every other objective parameter are rolled from ranges on the template row when the board rolls the offer.
40. Escort: accepting an Escort quest spawns an escort NPC (a Citizen row in `DT_Factions_NPCs`) next to the accepting player, and the escort NPC follows that player. The destination is a town slot (capital, town 1, or town 2) of the issuing kingdom, other than the issuing town, rolled with the offer like a Deliver destination (Requirement 41); for a questline step it is rolled on accept (Requirement 47). The objective completes when the escort NPC comes within the destination radius (tuning value) of the destination town's center (Requirement 15). The quest fails, is removed from the quest log, and the escort NPC despawns when the escort NPC dies, when the escort NPC is farther than the escort leash distance (tuning value) from the accepting player, or when the accepting character leaves the world. Escort NPCs are not saved. When a character enters a world (`specs/multiplayer/multiplayer.md` Requirement 16) with an active Escort quest, for example from a save written before a crash or an unexpected disconnect, that quest fails and is removed from the quest log.
41. Deliver: accepting a Deliver quest adds its quest item to the player's inventory. The destination is a town slot (capital, town 1, or town 2) of the same kingdom, other than the issuing town, rolled with the offer. The objective completes when the player turns in at a Quest Giver in the destination town.
42. Quests are accepted per character. Accepting a board offer adds the character's GUID to that offer's accepted character GUID set (Requirement 37), and a character whose GUID is in the set cannot accept the offer. The offer stays on the board for other characters. An accepted quest stays in the quest log when the board refreshes. The quest log holds at most 10 active quests (tuning value), and accepting is refused with "Quest log full" at the limit.
43. A player can abandon an active quest from the quest log. Abandoning removes the quest and its progress, removes its quest item, and despawns its escort NPC. Abandoning a board quest also removes the character's GUID from the offer's accepted character GUID set when that offer is still on a board in the current world, so the abandoned board quest can be accepted again only while its offer is still on the board. An abandoned questline quest can be accepted again from the capital's Quest Giver.
44. Quest credit:
    - Hunt: when an enemy dies, every player who has an active Hunt quest naming that enemy's row and is loot-eligible for the kill (`specs/combat-loot/combat-loot.md` Requirement 29) gains 1 progress.
    - Clear Camp: when a camp becomes cleared, every player who has an active Clear Camp quest naming that camp's faction and region and is loot-eligible for the kill of that camp's last enemy completes the objective.
    - Gather and Deliver: only the player who turns in receives credit.
    - Escort: only the accepting player receives credit.
45. Turn-in: a Hunt, Gather, Clear Camp, or Escort quest from a Quest Board turns in at any Quest Giver of the issuing kingdom, and a Deliver quest turns in at a Quest Giver in its destination town. On turn-in the player receives: gold rolled within the template's gold reward range; the template's reputation reward with the issuing kingdom; the template's character XP reward (`specs/character-progression/character-progression.md` Requirement 8); and, when the item reward chance roll succeeds, one item from the item reward list. A reward equipment item rolls rarity and affixes through `UNamecLootSubsystem` at an item level rolled uniformly within the item-level band of the kingdom's home region in `DT_World_Climates`.
46. Quest items have 0 weight, cannot be dropped, stored in containers, or sold, and are removed when their quest is turned in or abandoned (`specs/inventory/inventory.md` Requirement 24).
47. Each kingdom has a hand-made questline of 5 quests, stored in order in `DT_Factions_Questlines` (placeholder content). Each questline row has: kingdom, step number, quest type, objective parameters, and rewards, in the same form as a quest template. The capital's Quest Giver offers the kingdom's questline one step at a time, in order, and turns in its quests, except a Deliver step, which turns in at a Quest Giver in its destination town (Requirements 41 and 45). Objective parameter and reward ranges on a questline row are rolled when the character accepts the step, and the rolled values are stored with the active quest (Requirement 48). Step 5 (the finale) can be accepted only while the character's tier with that kingdom is Honored. Each character completes each step once. Active questline quests count toward the quest log limit.
48. Quest state is stored in `UNamecCharacterSave`: each active quest with its objective parameters, progress, and tracked flag, and the completed questline steps per kingdom. Quest objectives reference only kingdoms, town slots, `DT_Combat_Enemies` rows, item definitions, and Outlaw faction and region pairs, never a world-specific actor, so an active quest stays valid in every world. The quest log is the Quests tab of the Inventory and Character menu (`specs/game-foundation/game-foundation.md` Requirement 14), shown in the owning local player's viewport. The Quests tab lists active quests with objectives, progress, and rewards, the Track and Abandon actions, completed questline steps, and the Reputation section (Requirement 12).
49. The HUD quest tracker shows up to 3 tracked quests (tuning value) with their objective progress, in each local player's own viewport. The Track action is disabled while 3 quests are tracked.

### Raids
50. Raids happen only while the world setting "Raids" is on (on by default; `specs/voxel-world/voxel-world.md` Requirement 5).
51. A **base** is a group of building pieces (`specs/voxel-world/voxel-world.md` Requirement 24) built around anchors. Every crafting station (`specs/crafting-jobs/crafting-jobs.md` Requirement 7) and every bed is an anchor. Each anchor collects every building piece whose origin is within 30 m (tuning value) of that anchor. Anchors whose collected piece sets share at least one piece belong to the same base, and the base's pieces are the union of those sets. A group counts as a base only when it has at least 10 pieces (tuning value). The base center is the average of its piece origins, and the base area is the sphere around the base center that reaches the farthest piece origin.
52. Base value = the number of pieces in the base + 5 × the total tier of every crafting station in the base (coefficients in `DT_Factions_Raids`).
53. The server keeps one real-time raid roll clock per world. Every 5 real-time minutes (tuning value) on that clock, the server rolls a raid for each base that has at least one player within 100 m (tuning value) of its base center at that moment and no active raid. The raid roll clock measures real time, not in-game time (an exception to `specs/voxel-world/voxel-world.md` Requirement 11): it starts at 0 when the world loads, advances only while the world is not paused (`specs/multiplayer/multiplayer.md` Edge Cases 1 and 2), and a sleep time skip neither advances it nor triggers a roll. No roll happens when the Raids setting is off, while any boss arena is sealed with a player inside (`specs/combat-loot/combat-loot.md` Requirement 25), or during the world's first 3 in-game days (tuning value), measured by the world's elapsed in-game time, which a sleep time skip does advance. A roll skipped for any of these reasons does not change the clock, and the next roll comes at the next 5-minute mark. The factions that **can raid** are Bandits and Beastmen, always, plus every kingdom with which at least one player in the session has Hostile tier. Raid chance = `clamp(BaseChance + BaseValueCoefficient × BaseValue + Σ HostilityFactor, 0, 1)`, summing the HostilityFactor of every faction that can raid. BaseChance, BaseValueCoefficient, and each faction's HostilityFactor are in `DT_Factions_Raids`.
54. When the roll succeeds, the raider source is picked at random, weighted by HostilityFactor, among the factions that can raid plus the base's region monsters, whose weight is RegionMonsterWeight in `DT_Factions_Raids`. The candidate enemy rows are the `Hostile`-category rows in `DT_Combat_Enemies` whose region matches the base's region and whose Faction matches the source (`None` for region monsters). A faction, or the region monsters, with no candidate rows for the base's region is left out of the source pick. When no source has candidate rows, the raid does not start.
55. Raid size (number of raiders) and difficulty (enemy level offset) are rolled independently and uniformly within the band of the `DT_Factions_Raids` row for the base's region order (`specs/voxel-world/voxel-world.md` Requirement 8) and base value bracket. Each band row has a minimum and maximum raider count and a minimum and maximum level offset. Each raider is a candidate row picked at random, with level = the row's level + the level offset, and health and damage multiplied by the per-level multipliers in `DT_Factions_Raids` for each level of offset. Loot item level uses the offset level. Enemy player-count scaling (`specs/combat-loot/combat-loot.md` Requirement 21) applies to raiders on top of the level offset.
56. A **raider** is an enemy spawned by a raid. Raids give no warning: no HUD message, sound cue, or marker. Raiders spawn at raider spawn candidates: points on walkable terrain sampled at a raider spawn sample spacing of 5 m (tuning value) between the base area's edge and 100 m from the base center (tuning value). A candidate is valid when it is outside the base area, at least 40 m from every player (the player distance rule of `specs/combat-loot/combat-loot.md` Requirement 20, same tuning value), out of line of sight of every player's camera, and outside every town protected radius (Requirement 15). Raiders spawn at the valid candidates closest to the base area's edge. Raiders ignore the building piece distance rule, spawn density, and per-chunk caps of `specs/combat-loot/combat-loot.md` Requirement 20. When no valid candidate exists, the raid does not start.
57. Raiders target the building pieces in the raid's snapshot (Requirement 58) first (including crafting stations, storage containers, and beds) and the players who attack them, per `specs/combat-loot/combat-loot.md` Requirement 43. Raider attacks damage building pieces (`specs/voxel-world/voxel-world.md` Requirement 26). Destroyed storage containers drop their contents as shared world pickups (`specs/inventory/inventory.md` Edge Case 7), destroyed beds clear their respawn points (`specs/survival/survival.md` Requirement 18), and destroyed crafting stations cancel their queues (`specs/crafting-jobs/crafting-jobs.md` Edge Case 5).
58. When raiders spawn, the server snapshots the raid's building piece set: every building piece of the base at that moment, together with the base center at that moment (the raid's base center). During the raid, pieces join the snapshot only through a base merge (Edge Case 11). A raid ends when every raider is dead. A raid also ends, and every surviving raider retreats, when 10 real-time minutes (tuning value) have passed since the raiders spawned or when every piece in the snapshot is destroyed. Destroying anchors (beds or crafting stations) does not end the raid early and does not remove pieces from the snapshot. A retreating raider stops attacking, moves away from the base center, and despawns once it is out of line of sight of every player's camera or after the retreat time (tuning value), whichever comes first. The raid duration and the retreat time are measured in real time, not in-game time (an exception to `specs/voxel-world/voxel-world.md` Requirement 11), and advance only while the world is not paused. A sleep time skip cannot advance them, because sleeping cannot skip time while a raid is active. When no player is within 100 m of the raid's base center, the raid ends and every surviving raider despawns immediately. A base has at most one active raid at a time: a base any of whose pieces is in an active raid's snapshot is not rolled (Requirement 53). While any raid is active, sleeping cannot skip time (`specs/survival/survival.md` Edge Case 8).
59. Raids and the raid roll clock (Requirement 53) are not saved. An autosave does not change an active raid. When a world loads, no raid is active, and raiders from before the host exited are not restored.
60. Raider kills follow the normal kill rules: loot, gold, KillXP, and Hunt quest credit (`specs/combat-loot/combat-loot.md` Requirements 28 and 29).

### Tuning Data
61. Every value marked "tuning value" or "starting value" in this spec that names no other table lives in `DT_Factions_Rules`.

### Data Validation
62. An editor data validator (Data Validation, `specs/engine-tech/engine-tech.md` Requirement 3) fails the content build when a Bandits or Beastmen camp density in `DT_Factions_Factions` is above 0 for a region that has no `Hostile`-category row in `DT_Combat_Enemies` with that faction and region, so every camp spawn point can pick an enemy (Requirement 18).

### Hostile Recovery
63. At each daily 06:00 in-game tick (the Requirement 34 restock hour, including when a sleep time skip passes 06:00), every character in the session whose reputation with a kingdom is in the Hostile tier gains 2 reputation with that kingdom (tuning value), capped at −49 (tuning value, the lowest Unfriendly value). This recovery never raises reputation above −49 and never applies to Bandits or Beastmen (Requirement 6). The new value is the character's reputation, saved with it (Requirement 11), and the tier recomputes (Requirement 10).
64. Each capital has one **Guard Captain**, a `DT_Factions_NPCs` row with role Guard Captain placed at an NPC post of the capital (Requirement 16). A Guard Captain has no attack set, cannot be killed (Requirement 24), is not refused to Hostile-tier characters, and has no respawn time.
65. The fine screen (Requirement 27) shows the character's reputation with the Guard Captain's kingdom and, while that reputation is in the Hostile tier, the fine: `500 + 10 × (−49 − reputation)` gold (500 and 10 are tuning values). Pay is enabled only while the tier is Hostile and the character's gold is at least the fine. Paying removes the fine from the character's gold and sets reputation with that kingdom to −49 immediately (tier recomputes, Requirement 10). For 60 s after paying (tuning value), Guards of that kingdom within 20 m of that Guard Captain (tuning value) do not attack that player, including for earlier damage to them (Requirement 23). A Guard that the player damages during those 60 s attacks that player again. The server validates range, Hostile tier, and gold on every payment.

## Data Flow
1. Host creates a world → `UNamecWorldGenerator` places capitals, towns, camps, Quest Boards, and NPC posts from the seed → the world save initializes every Vendor's stock, every Quest Board's offers, empty NPC respawn timers, and empty camp states.
2. When a town's chunk streams in, `UNamecTownSubsystem` spawns an `ANamecTownNPC` at every post whose NPC has no running respawn timer, and an `ANamecQuestBoard` at the board position.
3. A player interacts with a Vendor → client sends `ServerBuyItem(VendorId, StockRowId, Quantity)` or `ServerSellItem(VendorId, ItemInstanceId, Quantity)` → server validates range, reputation tier, gold, stock, and ownership → `UNamecCurrencyComponent` changes gold, `UNamecInventoryComponent` adds or removes the item, `UNamecTownSubsystem` changes stock → stock replicates to every client, and gold replicates to the owning client.
4. A player accepts a board offer → client sends `ServerAcceptQuest(BoardId, OfferId)` → server validates reputation tier, quest log size, and that the character's GUID is not in the offer's accepted character GUID set → the server adds the GUID to that set, and `UNamecQuestComponent` adds the quest, and adds a quest item or spawns an escort NPC for Deliver and Escort quests.
5. An enemy dies → the server determines eligible players (`specs/combat-loot/combat-loot.md` Data Flow 4) → `UNamecQuestSubsystem` credits Hunt progress and, when the enemy was a camp's last, marks the camp cleared and completes matching Clear Camp objectives.
6. A player turns in at a Quest Giver → client sends `ServerTurnInQuest(QuestGiverId, QuestId)` → server validates location, tier, and completed objectives → `UNamecCurrencyComponent` adds gold, `UNamecReputationComponent` adds reputation, `UNamecProgressionComponent` adds character XP, and `UNamecLootSubsystem` rolls the item reward.
7. A player's damage reaches a town NPC → `UNamecReputationComponent::ApplyNPCHarm` applies the Requirement 8 loss → the tier recomputes → Guards update hostility.
8. At 06:00 in-game each day, `UNamecTownSubsystem` restocks every Vendor and refreshes every Quest Board.
9. Every 5 real-time minutes on the raid roll clock (Requirement 53), `UNamecRaidSubsystem` builds the base list from placed building pieces (Requirement 51), rolls each qualifying base (Requirement 53), and for each success picks the source, size, and difficulty, spawns raiders, and snapshots the base's building piece set and base center (Requirement 58) → raiders run their targeting until the raid ends (Requirement 58).
10. On save, each character's gold, reputation, and quest state write to `UNamecCharacterSave`, and the host writes NPC respawn timers, camp states, Vendor stock, Quest Board offers with their accepted character GUID sets, the Raids setting, and the world's elapsed in-game time to `UNamecWorldSave`.
11. At 06:00 in-game each day, `UNamecReputationComponent` applies Hostile recovery (Requirement 63) for every character in the session → the tier recomputes → Guards update hostility.
12. A Hostile-tier player interacts with a Guard Captain → the fine screen opens → client sends `ServerPayFine(GuardCaptainId)` → server validates range, Hostile tier, and gold → `UNamecCurrencyComponent` removes the fine → `UNamecReputationComponent` sets reputation to −49 → the tier recomputes → Guards of that kingdom within 20 m of the Guard Captain start the 60 s truce for that player (Requirement 65).

## Edge Cases
1. When a character save from before reputation, gold, and quests existed is loaded, the save migration (`specs/game-foundation/game-foundation.md` Requirement 7) sets the Requirement 6 starting reputation values, 0 gold, and an empty quest log.
2. When a player with Friendly tier and a player with Hostile tier stand together in a town, the Guards attack only the Hostile-tier player, and the Guards' attacks do not damage the Friendly-tier player.
3. When a player's area-of-effect ability damages a Citizen while fighting an enemy inside a town, the player loses reputation per Requirement 8 like any other attack.
4. When a player damages a Vendor, the Vendor cowers, its screen closes for every player using it, and the player loses 10 reputation (starting value). Further hits within the cooldown cost no further reputation.
5. When a player completes a Hunt kill in World A and joins World B, the progress is kept, and the quest turns in at any Quest Giver of the issuing kingdom in World B.
6. When two players buy the last unit of a stock row at the same time, the first request the server receives succeeds, and the second is rejected with "Out of stock".
7. When a player's gold is lower than the price, the buy is rejected with "Not enough gold".
8. When the escort NPC's path is blocked by terrain or building pieces, the escort NPC keeps following the accepting player, and the quest fails only by Requirement 40.
9. When a raid is active and a boss arena seals with a player inside, the active raid continues. Only new rolls are skipped.
10. When a base drops below 10 pieces during a raid, or loses every anchor, the raid continues against its snapshot until Requirement 58 ends it.
11. When a base merges with another group because a newly placed piece joins their anchor sets during a raid, every piece of the merged base joins the active raid's snapshot, the raid continues, and the merged base has no further roll until the raid ends. This is the only way pieces join a snapshot.
12. When a Hostile-tier player's teammate, who is Friendly with that kingdom, is in the session, raids by that kingdom can still target the teammate's base, because a kingdom can raid when any player in the session is Hostile with it.
13. When the host exits during a raid, the raid is not saved, and the world save written on host exit keeps every building piece destroyed and every container content dropped before the exit.
14. When a player with 10 active quests talks to the capital's Quest Giver, the next questline step is shown, and accepting it is refused with "Quest log full".
15. When a character is not in any session at 06:00 in-game, no Hostile recovery applies for that day, because recovery runs only for characters in the session (Requirement 63).
16. When a Hostile-tier player has less gold than the fine, Pay is disabled and reputation is unchanged. Reputation still recovers daily (Requirement 63).
17. When a raider kills or destroys the last anchor of a raided base, the remaining pieces in the raid's snapshot stay raid targets, and the raid ends only by Requirement 58.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] A new Sauren character has reputation +30 with the Sauren kingdom, 0 with the other five kingdoms, and −100 with Bandits and Beastmen.
- [ ] Killing a Guard as a Neutral-tier player reduces reputation with that kingdom by 35 (10 for the attack and 25 for the kill), and Bandits reputation never changes.
- [ ] A Hostile-tier player is attacked by Guards on sight and is refused by that kingdom's Vendors, Quest Givers, and Quest Boards.
- [ ] A character at −60 with a kingdom reaches −50 after 5 in-game days in the session, including days skipped by sleeping, reaches −49 on the next day, and never rises above −49 by recovery.
- [ ] A character at −60 pays a Guard Captain 610 gold and is set to −49, a character with 609 gold cannot pay, and Guards within 20 m of that Guard Captain do not attack the paying player for 60 s.
- [ ] Building is blocked inside a camp's radius while digging there still works, and a cleared camp respawns every enemy even with a player standing within 40 m of its spawn points.
- [ ] With CHA modifier +2 and Honored tier, an item with Value 100 costs `round(100 × 0.94 × 0.80)` = 75 gold, and sells for 25 gold as a Common item, 62 gold as a Rare item, and 200 gold as a Legendary item.
- [ ] Every generated world has one capital and 2 towns in each kingdom's home region, and none in Snowy Mountains or Volcanic.
- [ ] Digging, filling, building, and enemy spawning are all blocked inside a town's protected radius.
- [ ] A killed Citizen respawns at its post 1 in-game day later, including across save → quit → load.
- [ ] A Vendor's stock bought out by one player is empty for every other player, and restocks at 06:00 in-game.
- [ ] Gold dropped by one player is picked up by another player and added to that player's gold.
- [ ] A Quest Board shows 3–5 offers and refreshes at 06:00 in-game, and accepted quests stay in the quest log after the refresh.
- [ ] Two eligible players with the same Hunt quest active each gain progress from one kill.
- [ ] A character cannot accept a board offer it has already accepted, including after save → quit → load, and can accept it again after abandoning while the offer is still on the board.
- [ ] A character that enters a world with an active Escort quest in its save has that quest failed and removed from the quest log.
- [ ] A questline Deliver step turns in at a Quest Giver in its destination town, not at the capital.
- [ ] An Escort quest's destination is always another town slot of the issuing kingdom, and the objective completes when the escort NPC reaches that town's destination radius, in any world with the same town slot.
- [ ] A Guard's damage to a player is the same in a 1-player and a 4-player session.
- [ ] Quest progress, tracked quests, and completed questline steps survive save → quit → load and carry to another world.
- [ ] The questline finale cannot be accepted at Friendly tier and can be accepted at Honored tier.
- [ ] The HUD tracker shows up to 3 tracked quests in each split-screen viewport independently.
- [ ] A base of 10 pieces including a Workbench, with a player within 100 m, can be raided, and a group of 9 pieces cannot.
- [ ] Raiders spawn at least 40 m from every player, out of every player's line of sight, and never inside a town's protected radius.
- [ ] A raid on a base whose region has no candidate rows for a faction that can raid never picks that faction, and a content build with a camp density above 0 in a region with no matching camp enemy row fails validation.
- [ ] Raiders damage and destroy building pieces, and a destroyed chest drops its contents as shared world pickups.
- [ ] Destroying the bed and every crafting station of a raided base does not end the raid while other pieces in the snapshot stand, and destroying every piece in the snapshot makes surviving raiders retreat.
- [ ] No raid occurs during the world's first 3 in-game days, or with the Raids setting off.
- [ ] Surviving raiders retreat and despawn 10 real-time minutes after spawning, and no raid is active after save → quit → load.
- [ ] With a player standing within 100 m of a qualifying base, a raid roll happens every 5 real-time minutes, and a sleep time skip neither triggers a roll nor moves the next roll earlier.
